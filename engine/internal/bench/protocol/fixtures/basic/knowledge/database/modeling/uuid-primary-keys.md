New tables use application-generated UUIDv7 keys.

## Context
Sequential integers leaked volume and conflicted across shards.

## Decision
UUIDv7 for time-ordered locality.

## Alternatives
ULIDs needed a custom type.

## Consequences
Indexes are larger than with bigint keys.
